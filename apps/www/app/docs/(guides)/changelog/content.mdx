## What's new since 0.1.0

Everything below is in the changelog itself, entry by entry. In short:

- **Your style on top.** Your mood boards become styles (Design Eye), each product picks the one that
  fits (Style Fit), `style use` wears it and `style set` moves one setting. The slop police stops a
  style that reads as made by AI. Echo's library has Drafting Index beside Echo's own. [Styles](/docs/styles)
- **Light and drawn devices.** A pale chassis makes a light device, and `render: drawn` draws every
  part in the ink. Pills apart from the radius, a display size, and readability past AA.
  [Theming](/docs/theming)
- **Checks and tests.** The check in your code, the page check on the running page, Echo's tests
  with the design tests and axe, the Stop gate, and a job for every pull request.
  [Checks and tests](/docs/checks)
- **An agent that knows it's in Echo.** `init` writes the Echo block, `DESIGN.md`, the skills and the
  hooks, and `which` and `docs` answer an agent's questions offline. [For AI agents](/docs/ai)
- **The MCP server, hosted, and a plugin.** Ten tools, on your machine or at a hosted address, and
  Echo's plugin for Claude and ChatGPT. [MCP server](/docs/mcp), [Plugin](/docs/plugin)
- **Upgrades that keep your edits.** `diff` says what changed, and `update` merges Echo's change into
  the files you edited. [CLI](/docs/cli#update)
- **More components.** Dates, file upload, a table that sorts, selects, filters and windows, app
  navigation, the choosing controls, containers, the objects (dials, keypads, readouts), the motion
  family and the creature. [Components](/docs/components)
